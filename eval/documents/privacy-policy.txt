CLOUDSYNC PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: January 15, 2024

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Account Information
When you create a CloudSync account, we collect your name, email address, and password. For paid plans, we also collect billing information including credit card details (processed securely through Stripe).

1.2 File Content
We store the files and content you upload to CloudSync. This includes documents, images, videos, and any other files you choose to sync.

1.3 Usage Information
We automatically collect information about how you use CloudSync, including:
- Device information (operating system, browser type)
- IP addresses and location data
- File access logs and activity timestamps
- Feature usage statistics

1.4 Cookies
We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session, remember your preferences, and analyze site usage.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the CloudSync service
- Process your transactions and send billing notifications
- Send important service updates and security alerts
- Improve our products and develop new features
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

We will NEVER sell your personal information to third parties.

3. DATA RETENTION

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete files, they remain in your trash for 90 days before permanent deletion. After account closure, we retain your data for 30 days to allow account recovery, then permanently delete all your files and data.

Version history is retained according to your plan:
- Free plan: 30 days
- Professional plan: 180 days
- Enterprise plan: Custom retention periods

4. DATA SECURITY

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- AES-256 encryption for all data at rest
- TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- SOC 2 Type II certified data centers
- Two-factor authentication available for all users

5. DATA SHARING

We share your data only in these limited circumstances:
- With your explicit consent
- With service providers who help us operate CloudSync (AWS, Stripe)
- To comply with legal requirements (court orders, subpoenas)
- To protect our rights or prevent illegal activity

We do not share your file content with any third parties except as described above.

6. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and data
- Export your data (available in account settings)
- Opt out of marketing emails
- Restrict processing in certain circumstances

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@cloudsync.com.

7. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

CloudSync is based in the United States. If you access our service from outside the US, your data will be transferred to and stored in the US. We comply with applicable data protection laws including GDPR for EU users.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

CloudSync is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we discover we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or through the CloudSync app. Continued use of CloudSync after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
